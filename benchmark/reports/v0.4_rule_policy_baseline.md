## Remaining capability gaps

- word-number understanding in English and Chinese;
- common-name aliases such as zinc bromide and potassium bromide;
- informal low-stock and implicit sufficiency phrasing;
- `discard` and `expire` inventory event families;
- project extraction from free text.

All identified negation, future-intent, interrogative, conditional, range, inequality,
and unsupported multi-value numeric cases are held without an operational write.

Reproduce the report with:

```bash
chemclaw benchmark-run benchmark/cases \
  --output-dir outputs/v0.4-benchmark \
  --stem v0.4_rule_policy_baseline
```
