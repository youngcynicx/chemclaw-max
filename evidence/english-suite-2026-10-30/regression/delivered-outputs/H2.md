**Effect of modulator strength/loading and temperature — three-sample comparison (P01–P06 record)**

**The two levers varied**

| Lever | Zr-BDC-01 | Zr-BDC-02 | Zr-BDC-03 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modulator | Acetic acid (weak), 30 eq, 1.72 mL | TFA (strong), 30 eq, 2.30 mL | TFA (strong), **60 eq**, 4.60 mL |
| Water | 0.1 mL | 0.1 mL | **0.5 mL (5×)** |
| Temperature/time | 120 °C / 24 h | 120 °C / 24 h | **160 °C / 36 h** |
| Activation | 150 °C / 12 h | 150 °C / 12 h | **180 °C / 12 h** |

Note: 03 changes three variables at once (modulator loading, water, temperature/time, plus harsher activation). Comparisons 01↔02 isolate modulator *strength* at constant loading; 02↔03 changes strength-loading *and* water *and* temperature simultaneously — so 02→03 attribution is correlational, not single-variable.

**1. Modulator strength at fixed loading (01 → 02): beneficial, ordered defect engineering**
- Defects: missing linkers/Zr₆ rises 0.30 → 1.38 (TGA).
- Structure: BET 1180 → 1620 m²/g (+37%); V_total 0.48 → 0.74; ordered bimodal NLDFT 0.85 + 1.85 nm (1.85 nm defect cages); PXRD framework retained, FWHM only 0.14° → 0.18°, with the reo nanodomain diffuse hump (4.1°–5.8°).
- Separation: IAST 28.4 → 54.2; Qst⁰ 26.5 → 34.8 kJ/mol; CO₂ 298 K/1 bar 1.85 → 3.42 mmol/g; breakthrough 23.0 → 41.5 min/g. Open Zr⁴⁺ sites (recorded annotation) strengthen CO₂ quadrupole interaction; N₂ stays low (0.14–0.16), so selectivity gains come almost entirely from CO₂ side.
- Stability cost is modest: humid cycling 97.8% → 94.6% retention, fully recoverable at 150 °C ("framework intact").

**2. Pushing the same levers further, plus heat (02 → 03): collapse**
- Defects: 1.38 → 2.35 apparent missing/Zr₆; T_d drops 490 → 435 °C (early loss from 410 °C).
- Structure: crystallinity ~55% of 01, FWHM 0.42°, partial amorphization; fragmented SEM aggregates with cracks/pits; μ₃-OH severely depleted (dehydroxylation → Zr-O-Zr at 180 °C); BET collapses to 740 m²/g, micropore volume nearly halved (0.21 cm³/g); "mesopores" appear but as broad disordered 2.6–3.2 nm voids — porosity from etching/collapse, not ordered cages. Yield down to 162 mg (LOW YIELD).
- Separation: worst of the three (IAST 21.0, uptake 1.52 mmol/g at 298 K/1 bar, breakthrough 16.8 min/g, 94.0% dry retention). Residual defect affinity exists (Qst 29.1 > 01's 26.5) but there is too little intact micropore volume to exploit it.
- Humidity: irreversible failure — 38.5% uptake loss, BET → 430 m²/g, attributed to Zr-O-C hydrolysis at under-coordinated, dehydroxylated nodes.

**Volcano-type relationship? Yes — with caveats**

The data are consistent with a volcano plot of separation performance vs. defect concentration (proxy: missing linkers/Zr₆):

- 0.30 missing (01): too few sites — moderate BET (1180), IAST 28.4.
- 1.38 missing (02): peak — BET 1620, IAST 54.2, Qst 34.8, uptake 3.42.
- 2.35 missing (03): far side — collapse; IAST 21.0, lowest of all despite the most sites.

This is a classic volcano shape: performance rises as defects create open metal sites and hierarchical porosity, then falls as defect density exceeds the framework's connectivity threshold — beyond which crystallinity, microporosity, and hydrolytic stability degrade (03 also suffered compounded thermal stress from 160 °C/36 h synthesis and 180 °C activation).

Caveats (important for honest inference):
1. The three points vary multiple factors simultaneously (03 = 60 eq TFA + 5× water + 160 °C/36 h + 180 °C activation), so the descending branch cannot be attributed to modulator loading alone — over-hydrolysis and thermal stress are confounded contributors.
2. Three points define a trend, not a fitted curve; the true optimum could lie anywhere near 02's defect level.
3. Explanations (reo nanodomains, open Zr⁴⁺ sites, micropore collapse, irreversible hydrolysis) are the notebook recorders' interpretations and my synthesis of them — plausible and mutually consistent, but not proven mechanisms (e.g., ZrO₂ at 30.2° in 03 remains an unconfirmed possibility).

**Bottom line:** at fixed loading, switching from weak acetic acid to strong TFA takes UiO-66 from near-ideal to optimally defective and sharply improves CO₂/N₂ separation (28.4 → 54.2 IAST); doubling TFA to 60 eq with 5× water and 160 °C crosses the stability threshold, producing an amorphized, micropore-collapsed material with the worst performance and irreversible humidity damage. The results follow a volcano-type dependence on defect concentration, with 02 at the apex.
